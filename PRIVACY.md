# Pausely Privacy Policy

Pausely runs entirely on your device. It has no internet permission and sends no data anywhere.

- **Accessibility service:** used only to see which apps are on screen (package names from the window list), to time your use and to show pause/block screens. Android words this as "retrieve window content", but Pausely never reads text, messages or passwords.
- **Stored locally:** your limited-app list, limits, schedules, and 30 days of per-app usage minutes and open counts.
- **Removal:** uninstalling Pausely deletes all of it.
- **Export:** settings export is a file you create and control; it contains no usage history.
